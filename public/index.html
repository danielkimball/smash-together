Controller setup

Pair Bluetooth controllers in your computer’s settings, or connect by USB. Then press a button. Xbox controllers have a preset; use guided setup for N64 controllers and adapters.

Press buttons or move the stick

Your mapping is saved for this device.

Click any mapping to change it. Release the controls, then press the desired button or move an axis. Keyboard: press the desired key. Close this panel to resume control of the game.

A match in a few steps.

  1. Bring your cartridge.Choose the original US Smash 64 ROM on each computer. The game checks the file and remembers it in this browser.
  2. Settle into your controls.Use a paired Xbox controller, an N64 USB/Bluetooth controller, or the keyboard. Guided setup handles unusual adapters.
  3. Invite your rival.Power on, create a room, copy the invite and send it. Your friend opens it, loads their ROM, and joins. The host is Player 1.
  4. Head to VS Mode.Use Start to pass the title screen, choose VS Mode, and select your fighters. Your original game’s menus and rules work as usual.

Keep both game tabs visible. Wired internet helps. If play stalls, pause and resync; a larger connection buffer can help on distant connections.

The laptop hosting the server must stay awake. The sharing address may change when its tunnel restarts. No payment or account is required.
