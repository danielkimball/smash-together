Get together. Smash.

  1. Choose VS Mode / Multiplayer. Join a room, create one, or open your friend’s invitation.
  2. Load your own ROM. We check the original US release and keep it in your browser.
  3. Choose your fighter while you wait. Your room opens straight into the original character screen. Move your colored hand with the stick or WASD and press A / J to choose. Friends receive the next controller port when they join; your selection stays in place.
  4. Start the match. When everyone has chosen, the host presses Start / Enter or Start Match. The room closes to new arrivals during the fight and opens again in character selection.

Keyboard: WASD moves · J attacks / selects · K special / back · Space jumps · L grabs · O shields · Enter starts · Esc opens the menu. On a controller, press L + R + Start together for the menu.

Keep the game tab visible. The hosting computer must stay awake. This temporary sharing address may change when its tunnel restarts.
