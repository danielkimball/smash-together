Get together. Smash.

  1. Choose Multiplayer. Join a room, create one, or open your friend’s invitation.
  2. Load your own ROM. We check the original US release and keep it in your browser.
  3. Pick your port and ready up. Friends appear in the lobby as they join. Use an Xbox controller, an N64 controller or adapter, or the keyboard.
  4. Choose your fighter. The host starts when everyone is ready. You go straight to the game’s character selection. Move your colored hand to a fighter and press A. Press Start when everyone has chosen.

Keyboard: WASD moves · J attacks / selects · K special / back · Space jumps · L grabs · O shields · Enter starts · Esc opens the menu. On a controller, press L + R + Start together for the menu.

Keep the game tab visible. The hosting computer must stay awake. This temporary sharing address may change when its tunnel restarts.
